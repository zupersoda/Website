About us

A growth partner, not another vendor.

Zuper Soda is a growth consultancy built for small and mid-sized businesses. We connect your marketing with your sales, so every campaign, post and conversation is working toward the same number: profit. Rather than handing over a report and moving on, we stay close to the work, guide your team through it, and track the results until they actually show up in your numbers.

Note from the founder

Hi, I'm Shana.

Thank you for considering Zuper Soda.

Zuper Soda is a very personal project to me. I always get excited and interested talking to business owners, and this is my attempt to solve the common issues faced by small to mid-sized business owners.

I started as an engineer, not a marketer. I spent two years as a Product Consultant before I moved into marketing strategy, and eventually became COO at a marketing agency. Along the way I kept seeing the same gap. Businesses doing marketing well, with nothing to show for it in their sales numbers. That gap is why Zuper Soda exists.

If you're ambitious, and confident about your product or service, you've reached the right place.

We're new. I won't pretend otherwise. But I'd rather be honest about that than pretend to be something we're not.

If that sounds like the kind of partner you want, I'd love to hear from you.

Shana Ramla, Founder

How we work

Here's what working with Zuper Soda looks like, step by step. We take on only 5 clients each month.

  1. 01
    You find Zuper SodaA small to mid-sized business owner realises profits are not where they should be, and something is missing. They reach out directly to us or fill in our .
  2. 02
    We get in touchWe have a chat to understand your business and see if we're a good fit. We also talk about our next available slot.
  3. 03

  4. 04

  5. 05
    We set SMART goalsWe work fully based on SMART goals: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound. We set SMART goals at this stage.
  6. 06

  7. 07
    Review SMART goalsWe review the goals set every month. Sometimes we achieve them, sometimes we don't. The key is tracking, and our team constantly works towards achieving that goal.
  8. 08
    Goal achieved. Time for cheers! 🥂We celebrate the wins together. And then, there's always something new to work towards.
